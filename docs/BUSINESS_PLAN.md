# FlowForge AI — Business plan

## Positioning
A small-business workflow automation studio that helps service businesses turn repetitive intake, support, and meeting preparation tasks into human-reviewed drafts.

## Ideal customers
Local service contractors, property managers, consultants, professional service firms, and small internal operations teams.

## Starter offers (hypothetical prices, not market quotes)
- Automation discovery session: $150 one time
- Single workflow setup and documentation: $750–$1,500
- Ongoing workflow maintenance and support: $99–$299/month per client (define service boundaries)

## First 30 days
Week 1: Run prototype with fictional data, confirm safety, build demos.
Week 2: Interview five potential customers and identify a repeatable bottleneck.
Week 3: Deliver a small pilot with clear scope, contract, and manual approval.
Week 4: Document outcome, iterate pricing and service packages, seek testimonials with permission.

## Distinction
This repo is a functional prototype and demonstration of the business offering; it is not proof of legal entity formation, customer acquisition, or recurring revenue.
